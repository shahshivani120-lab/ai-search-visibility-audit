## Why this matters

Buyers now ask AI assistants questions like *"best performance management software for law firms"* before they ever visit a website. If your brand isn't in those answers, you're not on the shortlist. Classic SEO reports don't show this, so you need to check it directly.

## How to use it

1. **Define the category and competitors.** List 3–5 competitors and the buyer questions that matter (use the prompt library).
2. **Run the prompts** in each AI platform, in a clean or logged-out session where possible. Log results in `citation-tracker.csv`.
3. **Analyse the gaps.** Where is the brand missing? Which sources get cited instead (competitors, review sites, Reddit, publications)?
4. **Check the site.** Use the on-site section of the checklist: crawlability, content formats, schema, clarity of claims.
5. **Write the report** with `03-report-template.md` and a prioritised fix list.
6. **Re-test monthly.** AI answers change often, so track the trend, not one snapshot.

## What tends to get cited

From hands-on testing, AI assistants repeatedly quote content that:

- **Answers a specific buyer question directly** in the first lines (e.g. "Is X good for law firms?")
- **Compares options fairly**, such as "X vs Y" pages and buyer guides
- **Contains original data or research** that others can't copy
- **Is clearly structured**, with descriptive headings, short paragraphs, tables and FAQs
- **Is backed by third-party mentions**, such as publications, associations and credible backlinks

## Tools that help

- **Bing Webmaster Tools → AI Performance:** citations of your pages in Microsoft Copilot and partner AI answers
- **Google Search Console:** impressions and clicks for the same buyer queries
- **GA4:** referral sessions from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai and others, and how engaged those visitors are
