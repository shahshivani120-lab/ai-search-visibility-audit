# 03 · Audit Report Template

Copy this template into a doc for each client or brand. Lines in *italics starting with "Guide:"* explain what to write. Delete them in the final report.

**Tips for a good report:**

- Lead with the business impact, not the method. Executives read the summary and the fix list.
- Use numbers wherever possible (mention rates, citation shares, counts).
- Show 2–3 real screenshots of AI answers. They make the problem real.
- Keep the fix list short and prioritised. Ten clear actions beat fifty vague ones.
