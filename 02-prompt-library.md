# Buyer Prompt Library

Replace the placeholders:
`[category]` e.g. performance management software · `[audience]` e.g. law firms · `[brand]` · `[competitor]` · `[problem]` e.g. associate retention · `[market]` e.g. Australia

Test the same prompts on every platform so results are comparable.

## Awareness: the buyer is exploring a problem

- What causes [problem] in [audience]?
- How do [audience] usually solve [problem]?
- What are the biggest trends in [category] for [audience] this year?
- Why does [problem] matter for [audience]?
- What does good [category] look like for [audience]?

## Consideration: the buyer is building a shortlist

- What is the best [category] for [audience]?
- Top [category] tools for [audience] in [market]
- What should [audience] look for when choosing [category]?
- Which [category] integrates with [tool, e.g. QuickBooks or Xero]?
- Affordable [category] for small [audience]
- Alternatives to [competitor] for [audience]

## Comparison: the buyer is choosing between options

- [brand] vs [competitor]: which is better for [audience]?
- [competitor] vs [brand]
- Is [competitor] good for [audience]?
- Pros and cons of [brand]
- What do [audience] say about [brand]?

## Decision: the buyer is checking details

- How much does [brand] cost?
- Is [brand] worth it for [audience]?
- How long does it take to set up [brand]?
- Does [brand] support [feature]?
- Who uses [brand]?

## Brand accuracy checks

- What is [brand]?
- Who is [brand] for?
- What does [brand] do differently from [competitor]?
- Is [brand] still available / actively developed?

## Tips

- Keep the wording natural, the way a real buyer would type it.
- Include the market or industry when buyers would (e.g. "for law firms", "in Australia").
- Prompts with comparison and "best X for Y" intent usually show the biggest gaps, so start there.
- Save the full answer text for important prompts so you can compare month to month.
