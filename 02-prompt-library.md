## 1. How to use this library

1. Replace the placeholders with your brand's details.
2. Pick 20–30 prompts using the funnel mix in `01-audit-checklist.md` (step 1.5).
3. Keep the **exact same prompts** every month so results are comparable.
4. Log every result in `citation-tracker.csv`.

## 2. Placeholders

| Placeholder | Meaning | Example (B2B SaaS) | Example (restaurant) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `[brand]` | The brand being audited | Artemis | Daawat Indian Restaurant |
| `[competitor]` | A named competitor | Lattice | Another local restaurant |
| `[category]` | What buyers call the product | performance management software | Indian restaurant |
| `[audience]` | Who buys it | law firms | families, couples |
| `[problem]` | The pain it solves | associate turnover | finding a good dinner spot |

| `[market]` | Location | US, Australia | Brisbane CBD |
| `[size]` | Company size | 200-lawyer firm | group of 10 |

## 11. Writing your own prompts

Good prompts sound like real buyers. Sources for real phrasing:

- **Google Search Console → Performance → Queries:** especially long queries and questions
- **"People also ask"** boxes in Google results
- **Sales and support call notes:** the exact words customers use
- **Reddit and forum threads** in your category
- **Review sites:** what reviewers compare you against
- **Bing Webmaster Tools → AI Performance:** the queries where your pages are already cited

**Checklist for a good prompt:**

- ☐ Sounds like something a real person would type or say
- ☐ Includes the audience or context when buyers would ("for law firms", "in Brisbane")
- ☐ Doesn't include your brand name, unless it's a comparison, decision or accuracy prompt
- ☐ Asks one thing at a time
- ☐ Is neutral (no "Why is [brand] the best?" leading questions)

## 12. How to run prompts properly

1. **New chat for every prompt.** Previous messages change the answer.
2. **Web search on.** Check for a search/browse toggle.
3. **Logged out or memory off.** Avoid personalised results.
4. **Same location each month.** Note it in the tracker.
5. **Copy the prompt exactly.** Small wording changes can change the answer.
6. **Repeat the top 5 prompts 2–3 times.** Record e.g. "2/3" for consistency.
7. **Save evidence.** Screenshots or copied text for key prompts, named like `2026-11-19_chatgpt_prompt13.png`.
8. **Don't argue with the AI during the test.** Follow-ups are useful for research, but keep them out of the scored results.
